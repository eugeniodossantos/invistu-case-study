# Features — INVISTU

## Financial diagnosis

- Initial financial assessment
- Income and commitment mapping
- Financial-risk visibility
- Starting recommendations based on the user's reality

## Daily financial management

- Recurring and additional income
- Expense registration and categorization
- Debt tracking
- Priorities and responsibilities
- Financial movements and history

## Planning

- Savings planning
- Emergency fund
- Financial reserves
- Goals
- Simulations before major decisions
- Unexpected-expense planning

## Guidance

INVISTU is designed to be proactive. The product can surface warnings and recommendations when the available financial data suggests that a planned decision may harm stability or delay important goals.

## Multi-context workspaces

- Personal
- Family
- Company

Each workspace keeps financial data, responsibilities and permissions separated.

## Account and security

- Profile / identity settings
- Security controls
- Device and session visibility
- Privacy settings
- Activity / audit history

## Reporting

- Financial summaries
- Progress against goals
- Income / expense visibility
- Planning indicators
- Workspace-specific reports

## Product design principles

- Simple user flows despite complex financial rules
- Mobile-friendly and responsive interfaces
- Clear prioritization of financial risk
- Actionable information instead of raw data only
- API-first architecture for web and mobile evolution
